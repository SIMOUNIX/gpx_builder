# GPX builder

Plans multi-day hiking trips and turns them into GPX routes built from OpenStreetMap data.

## Along the route

**POI**:
A point of interest from OpenStreetMap near a stage's route: water, food, picnic or resupply.
_Avoid_: place (a place is a start, end or overnight location found by name)

**Stop**:
A POI the route passes through, to take a break or fetch something: either on the path or reached by a short detour that loops back to the route. A POI that is only displayed is not a stop.
_Avoid_: break, halt

**Detour**:
How much a stage's distance and walking time change when it is routed through a POI. Usually extra, sometimes negative when the path through the POI is shorter.
_Avoid_: gap (reserved for the distance between water sources), offset

**Detour allowance**:
The largest detour that still counts as near the route: 1% of the trip's length, at least 200 m and at most 2 km.

## Water

**Potable water**:
A water source safe to drink as is, such as a tap or a fountain.

**Treatable water**:
Natural water (stream, river, waterfall, lake, pond, spring not marked drinkable) that is usable after filtering or treating it.

**Unknown water**:
A tap or source whose drinkability is not recorded.
_Avoid_: non-potable (it may well be drinkable)

## Planning

**Candidate**:
A POI within the detour allowance, offered but not yet picked. Shown as an outlined icon; becomes a stop when picked.

**Walking time**:
The DIN 33466 estimate for a stage or a detour: 4 km/h horizontal, 300 m/h up, 500 m/h down; the larger of horizontal and vertical time plus half the smaller.
_Avoid_: duration, ETA

**Water treatment**:
The brief flag saying the hiker carries a filter or treatment. It decides whether treatable water counts for the `water-gap` rule. Defaults to no.

**Stage description / Trip description**:
Text generated by the app from the trip's facts (stops, water tiers, daily summary). Never written by the model.

**Planner note**:
The optional one-line comment the planner adds to a stage description. Advice only, never facts.
